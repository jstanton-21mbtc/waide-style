Our Story

Built for the
Craft

Respecting the past.

Waide Style is the realization of a vision years in the making. Owner Vinny (Gavin Waide) is dual-trained in barbering and cosmetology, and spent his formative years honing his craft at Anderson 56 alongside Chuck — a place that set the standard for what great barbering looks like in Avon.

That foundation is what Waide Style was built on. Now operating at 114 Genesee St, Vinny has opened his own shop on his own terms — and Chuck has joined him, carrying that same commitment to the craft forward.

“I look forward to having you in my chair.” — Vinny
