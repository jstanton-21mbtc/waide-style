Our Story

Built for the
Craft

Waide Style was built on a simple belief — every person deserves to walk out feeling sharp. Located on Main Street in Geneseo, NY, we blend old-school barbershop tradition with modern salon expertise under one roof.

Owner and master barber Gavin Waide opened this shop to create more than just a haircut. It’s a clean, premium experience where every detail matters and every client is treated right.
